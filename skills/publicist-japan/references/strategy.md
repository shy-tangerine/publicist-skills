# Japan media strategy

## Media system and channel choice

Japanese national and regional newspapers, broadcasters, specialist trade press, magazines, web-native outlets, podcasts, and local desks have different calendars and intake norms. Start with the beat and audience, not circulation. Use a wire only when a release has verifiable news value and the provider’s acceptance and disclosure rules fit; Kyodo PR Wire describes domestic and overseas distribution, but distribution is not coverage. A press club is a source-access institution, not a shortcut to editorial placement.

## Relationship-first practice

Build a small, beat-specific list from public bylines. Offer a useful source, data, translation support, and realistic response times. Lead with relevance and evidence; avoid mass CC, repeated nudges, attachments on first contact, and calls to personal numbers. A Japanese-language draft should be reviewed by a fluent human for register, nuance, and claims. Use JST and Japanese date conventions where helpful, while retaining an unambiguous ISO date in records.

## Foreign startups and experts

Credible entry points include a Japanese partner or customer who can speak independently, transparent case evidence, an FPCJ-eligible foreign correspondent route, specialist trade media, and JETRO or industry networks. Explain why the development matters to Japan rather than merely announcing entry. Ensure the spokesperson can answer in Japanese or provide an interpreter. Never imply local presence, traction, regulation approval, or customer endorsement that is not documented.

## Timing and follow-up

Ask the outlet about its editorial calendar when publicly invited; otherwise pitch with adequate lead time and a concrete expiry. Observe embargoes exactly and record JST. One polite follow-up through the approved channel may be reasonable; silence is not consent. Newsjacking, crisis, elections, and health/science claims require rapid human and subject-matter review.

## Deliberate anti-patterns

Do not buy undisclosed coverage, fabricate Japanese credentials, translate a quote to change its meaning, scrape journalist directories, evade press-club rules, or send bulk “personalized” mail. If a platform or club’s access terms are unclear, retain the lead as `NEEDS_REVIEW` and seek permission or counsel.
