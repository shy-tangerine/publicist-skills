# Contact providers for Japan

Read this file only after verifying the journalist, outlet, beat, and recent work from editorial sources.

## Primary route: PRONE / PRM media list

[PRONE's PRM Media List](https://prone.jp/prm) is a Japan-specific media-relations product. It combines a company's own journalist contacts with searchable public journalist information. Its official product page describes fields for name, outlet, beat/category, career, articles, social profiles, notes, and contact history, plus segmented email and engagement tracking.

Use it when the user's PRONE account exposes the relevant function:

1. Search public journalist information by the story's topic, then verify the role and recent work on the outlet's own site.
2. Keep user-owned contacts separate from PRONE public-profile data. Record `provider=PRONE`, lookup date, and which class supplied each field.
3. Treat public articles and social profiles as discovery evidence. Use an email or phone number only when its source and intended professional use are clear.
4. Prefer the outlet's inquiry form, editorial desk, PR wire, press-club route, or an existing introduction when that is the normal route.
5. PRONE can send segmented email and log opens/interactions. Present the exact recipient list and copy before using those functions.

PRONE's [business-user terms](https://prone.jp/terms) require account-level agreement, and its [privacy policy](https://prone.jp/privacy) governs user data. A 2024 product announcement reported more than 300 public journalist profiles at that time; treat that as historical evidence, not a current inventory or a comprehensive directory.

## What is not established

The official sources reviewed do not establish a comprehensive Japan-wide exportable journalist-email database comparable to a sales-contact database. Do not substitute Hunter, Clay, or Apollo as if they had demonstrated Japanese newsroom coverage. They may be used only as clearly labeled fallback enrichment for a known person when the Japan workflow permits it.

## Minimum output

Return name, outlet, beat, fit evidence, preferred route, provenance (`published`, `PRONE-owned`, `PRONE-public`, `provider-supplied`, or `inferred`), verification date, and next step. Use `UNRESOLVED` when evidence is missing.

Optional background: [localized media-contact infrastructure](https://github.com/shy-tangerine/publicist-skills/blob/main/wiki/public-relations/localized-media-contact-infrastructure.md). The installed guidance above is sufficient for the workflow.
