# Germany law and ethics routing (not legal advice)

This is operational risk guidance, not a legal opinion. Apply the facts, current text and competent counsel.

## Email, phone and direct marketing

§7 UWG makes unreasonable harassment unlawful; electronic mail advertising generally requires prior express consent, with a narrow existing-customer exception. Telephone requirements differ for consumers and other market participants. A public email address is not proof of consent. GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and Recital 47 can support a legitimate-interest analysis for direct marketing, but DSK guidance distinguishes GDPR processing from UWG consent rules. Art. 21(2)-(3) requires a prominent objection route and stopping marketing after objection.

## Identity, transparency and data minimization

Check the sender’s legal identity and contact details. §5 DDG requires business digital services to keep name/address and rapid electronic contact information easily recognizable, directly reachable and continuously available; register, regulator and VAT details may also apply. Keep only data needed for the approved task, record source and date, define retention, restrict access and honor objections/deletions where applicable. Never infer private contact details.

## Editorial ethics

The Deutscher Presserat’s Pressekodex emphasizes truth, dignity, care, correction and separation of editorial content from commercial interests. Do not offer gifts or pressure for coverage. Clearly label sponsored/paid content and disclose conflicts. PR material is a lead, not independent verification.

## Provider and cross-border uncertainty

Use only an authorized provider route and read current terms, API limits, export restrictions, anti-spam rules and data-processing/cross-border terms. For zimpel, the current [AGB](https://www.newsaktuell.de/agb/zimpel/) reviewed 2026-09-21 limits exported system data outside the application to one use within at most two days, prohibits durable external storage, and requires the customer's prior consent before emailing bloggers or social-media influencers. `robots.txt` is not permission. Do not upload journalist or client personal data to a new service without checking purpose, controller/processor roles, transfer safeguards and retention. If the route, consent, balancing test, transfer or deletion behavior is unclear: `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
