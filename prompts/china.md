Use `$publicist-china`. Start with the [shared brief](shared.md#shared-brief), then use the prompt below with your facts and sources. These prompts request drafts; sending requires separate approval.

## Research and pitch

> 针对[中国大陆地区/行业]的[新闻事实]制定媒体方案。先核实媒体、记者或公众号运营方的当前身份、近期相关署名、投稿渠道、发布时间和北京时间截止日期；把编辑报道、企业供稿、付费合作和分发分开。为前三个目标各写一个简体中文标题和简短采访提案：第一段说明本地影响，随后提供可核验的本地数据、专家或资产、方法限制和一个明确请求。不要把全球公告、数据库命中或付费分发写成中国报道。记录渠道和来源类别，并检查 PIPL 目的/必要性、海外处理、平台权限和敏感行业问题；未解决即 `NEEDS_REVIEW`。按原请求或官方渠道发送前只准备一条 3–5 个中国工作日后的有用跟进。

## Language and channel

> Classify the destination as a newsroom email/form, a live source-request
> channel, or a WeChat public account before drafting. For a newsroom, use a
> concise news lead, evidence, limitation, and interview/material request. For
> WeChat, check the account's published submission convention and whether the
> proposal is editorial, `企业供稿`, `付费合作`, or `分发`; use the account's
> natural short-form register and never imply that a public account accepts
> unsolicited bulk messages. Preserve verified Chinese names and avoid literal
> idioms, state-affiliation assumptions, or claims that a database result is
> coverage.

## Worked example


These are invented training cases. Outlet names, people, articles, dates, numbers, and URLs are placeholders, not claims about real contacts. In a real run, replace the bracketed evidence with a verified recent-work URL and use the market skill's route and legal gate.


The fictional brief follows.

A fictional Shenzhen manufacturing consortium has permission to publish a Chinese-language survey of 63 factories; the result concerns maintenance downtime, not a national market claim. Fictional target: the editor of *华南智造观察*, with verified recent coverage [URL/date] on factory data quality and an official submission route [URL].

The pitch follows.

> 标题：深圳63家工厂的设备停机数据：维护投入如何影响交付
>
> 编辑您好，贵栏目在[日期、URL]中关注了工厂数据质量对交付判断的影响。我们获得授权的深圳63家工厂调查显示，[经核验的结果]；样本范围、调查时间和不能外推到全国的限制见[方法链接]。我们可以提供脱敏数据表、调查方法，并安排一位能用中文说明维护记录的工程师。请问这项材料是否适合进一步沟通？如需核对，我可在北京时间[时间]通过贵方官方投稿渠道回复。本项目与[公司/机构]的关系为[披露]，不主张付费报道。

Why it works: it gives a local, bounded sample; distinguishes editorial material from paid placement; names a verified route; and preserves disclosure and scope.
