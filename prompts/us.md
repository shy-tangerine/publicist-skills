Use `$publicist-us`. Start with the [shared brief](shared.md#shared-brief), then use the prompt below with your facts and sources. These prompts request drafts; sending requires separate approval.

## Research and pitch

> Find current US source requests and proactive targets for [topic]. Use only the request's reply route or a professional route published by the newsroom or journalist. For each target, verify current role, beat, deadline in ET, CT, MT, or PT, format, and up to five recent bylines; cite URLs and dates. Rank only targets whose recent work shows the precise question this brief can answer. Draft one concise English pitch per top target: lead with the news, answer a source request before credentials, offer [asset/spokesperson], state one limitation, and ask for [specific next step] by [timezone]. Check platform human-authorship rules, CAN-SPAM classification where relevant, contact provenance, claim map, and `DRAFT_ALLOWED` gate. Return one useful follow-up after 3–5 business days and then stop.

## Worked example


These are invented training cases. Outlet names, people, articles, dates, numbers, and URLs are placeholders, not claims about real contacts. In a real run, replace the bracketed evidence with a verified recent-work URL and use the market skill's route and legal gate.


The fictional brief follows.

A fictional company has a permitted, independently audited dataset showing [18% fewer refrigeration failures in 42 Ohio grocery stores over 2025–26], with method and exclusions attached. An operations engineer can take a 20-minute call in ET. Fictional target: Dana Ruiz, *Lake Erie Ledger*, whose verified recent article [URL/date] examined supermarket food waste after a power outage.

The pitch follows.

> Subject: Data from 42 Ohio stores: what prevented refrigeration loss this winter
>
> Dana, your [verified article, date, URL] showed how one outage becomes a food-waste problem for Ohio grocers. We have an independently audited dataset from 42 stores covering 2025–26 that found 18% fewer refrigeration failures after [intervention], with the sample and exclusions here: [method link]. Our operations engineer [name/role] can explain the failure logs and where the data does not generalize. Would a 20-minute background call this week help your reporting? I can send the raw table and connect you with a participating store only with its permission.

Why it works: local evidence answers the reporter's existing question, offers inspectable material, states a limit, and asks for a small action.
