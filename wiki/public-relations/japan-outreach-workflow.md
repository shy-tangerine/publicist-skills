# Japan outreach workflow

> Sources: Nihon Shinbun Kyokai, 2002-03-09; Foreign Press Center Japan, Unknown; Japan Public Relations Society, Unknown; JETRO, Unknown
> References: [Kisha Club Guidelines](https://pressnet.or.jp/english/about/guideline/); [Foreign Press Registration Card](https://fpcj.jp/en/assistance/fpregcard/); [PRSJ guideline](https://prsj.or.jp/pr-guideline/); [JETRO portal](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/)
> Updated: 2026-09-12

## Overview

Japan outreach works best with a small verified beat list, useful evidence, locally relevant framing, and culturally competent Japanese copy. Press-club, event, direct editorial, and wire routes serve different purposes. The installed Japan skill contains the complete workflow; this article provides deeper background.

## Sequence

1. Gather the Japan-specific news value, verified facts and names, spokesperson language ability, JST availability, local partner permissions, exclusions, and prior relationships.
2. Search official staff pages, original bylines, inquiry pages, PRONE/PRM when available, FPCJ resources, and appropriate event or wire routes.
3. Verify outlet, journalist or desk, exact Japanese name, beat, recent work, contact route, deadline, and access conditions. Mark unknowns `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
4. Create one to three non-generic Japan-relevant angles and map each material claim to evidence.
5. Draft with the published name, correct use of `様` or `御中`, JST availability, affiliation disclosure, concise evidence links, and preserved caveats.
6. Return the ranked targets, fit evidence, preferred route, angles, draft, missing facts, and next step.
7. If the user asks to send, show the exact recipient, channel, copy, and timing immediately before the external action and obtain the confirmation required by the host.

## 最低限の記録

対象ごとに、公開どおりの氏名、媒体・編集部、最近の記事 URL と日付、公式窓口と出所、地域・読者、形式、JST の締切、取材アクセス条件、所属開示、主張の出典を保存する。本人確認、記者クラブの参加条件、メール区分、APPI、越境処理、禁輸条件が不明なら `NEEDS_REVIEW` とする。
