## Overview

Germany’s channel mix is regional, specialist and digital. Discovery should optimize for editorial fit and verifiable access, not an undifferentiated mass list.

## A repeatable record

For each candidate record outlet, Ressort, geography, author role, recent relevant article, public profile/Impressum URL, observed date, request wording, deadline, requested format and reply channel. Separate hard requirements from soft fit. Use IVW distribution or usage data as one reach signal only: it does not establish editorial quality, topic fit or willingness to publish.

The Medienanstalten describe media diversity as central to free opinion formation and monitor developments across providers and digital media. That supports deliberate selection of regional and specialist channels when their audience matches the story. The DJV source discipline supports checking recent work and corroborating claims rather than treating PR as evidence.

## Acquisition boundary

Use only public, authorized or contractually permitted routes. Do not infer personal addresses, scrape gated databases or export lists. A candidate record is research output, not permission to contact.
