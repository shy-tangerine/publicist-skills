## What changes in practice

The same dossier can produce five different next actions. A US source request may be answered on its originating platform. A German story may require a named `Ressort` and a current `Impressum` route. A mainland China assignment may need separate checks for a journalist request, a verified WeChat account, and cross-border processing. A Japanese event story may turn on press-club or Foreign Press Center eligibility before a pitch is useful. A Brazilian launch may fail without state-level consequence, correct Portuguese, or a route that respects LGPD.

Across all five, the invariant is editorial verification: current role, recent relevant work, fit, source provenance, and a correction path. The variable is the system used to discover and contact the person. A provider match, deliverability label, open metric, public account, or wire distribution never proves willingness to publish or independent coverage.
