# Japan PR wires and release quality

> Sources: Kyodo News PR Wire, 2023-04-01; Kyodo News PR Wire, Unknown; Japan Public Relations Society, Unknown; Japanese Psychological Association, Unknown

> Updated: 2026-09-12

## Overview

Kyodo PR Wire’s service guide describes distribution of press releases to media in Japan and abroad, while its acceptability standard sets content conditions. These are distribution mechanisms, not editorial guarantees. PRSJ and the Japanese Psychological Association provide useful ethical and evidence-quality context: responsible communication and careful explanation of research limits matter more than promotional language.

## Working rules

Before using a wire, verify current terms, prohibited content, sender identity, claims, rights to images, and whether paid distribution is labeled. Keep a release factual, attributable, and specific; distinguish research findings from interpretation and disclose limitations. A wire hit must never be reported as independent coverage. Use a direct, beat-relevant pitch when a newsroom needs context that a release cannot provide.
