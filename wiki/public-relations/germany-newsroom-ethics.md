# Germany: newsroom ethics and source discipline

## Overview

German editorial norms prioritize truth, human dignity, care, correction and a visible separation between editorial and commercial interests. PR is a lead for reporting, never a substitute for independent checking.

## Practical implications

Pressekodex Ziffer 2 requires information intended for publication to be checked for truth and reproduced faithfully; Ziffer 3 requires prompt, appropriate correction of false claims. Ziffer 7 calls for separation of editorial text and advertising, while Ziffer 15 cautions against gifts and benefits that impair independence. A pitch should therefore show source links, methods, limitations, attribution and a reachable person for questions, without offering inducements or demanding coverage.

The DJV warning that police reports are one source among several illustrates the same discipline: even an apparently official account is not automatically the truth. Label client claims as claims, provide counter-evidence and avoid laundering marketing copy through authority.

## Escalation

Potential conflict, paid placement, undisclosed sponsorship, unverifiable quote, embargo dispute or correction request requires a human decision and may require legal/editorial review.
