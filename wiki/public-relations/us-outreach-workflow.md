# United States media outreach workflow

> Sources: Society of Professional Journalists, 2014-09-06; Federal Trade Commission, Unknown
> References: [SPJ Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/); [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
> Updated: 2026-09-13

## Operating sequence

Classify national, local, regional, trade, broadcast, podcast, newsletter, and independent digital targets. Search first-party pages with `site:outlet.example topic reporter`, `site:outlet.example topic editor`, `topic city newsroom`, and `topic podcast`; record the outlet, desk, recent byline URL/date, deadline timezone, format, route, and provenance. Source-request platforms are reply channels, not reusable lists.

Use one specific recent article in the opening, offer a named source or checkable asset, state limitations, and distinguish editorial, sponsored, and wire distribution. Keep a correction route and honor opt-outs. A single useful follow-up after 3–5 business days is sufficient; stop when the opportunity expires or the recipient declines.

## Evidence gate

`DRAFT_ALLOWED` requires current role evidence, a first-party route or authorized provider record, a fit reason, and claim-to-source links. CAN-SPAM obligations concern commercial email and do not establish editorial permission; where classification or sender obligations are unclear, mark `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
