## Overview

An effective Germany-specific pitch is concise, attributable, useful to a named `Ressort`, and grounded in a clear German news peg. The installed Germany skill contains the complete working sequence; this article supplies deeper drafting context.

## Draft shape

Use a precise German subject, professional salutation, one news thesis, one concrete offer (`O-Ton`, interview, data, case, or document), evidence links, realistic availability, and a complete signature. Use German media terms accurately: `Redaktion`, `Ressort`, `Pressemitteilung`, `Ansprechpartner`, `O-Ton`, `Sperrfrist`, and `Beleg`.

Avoid guaranteed-coverage language, unsupported superlatives, and vague disruption claims. If content is paid or sponsored, label it and do not represent it as independent editorial coverage.

## Identity and timing

Confirm the sender's legal identity and reachable details. DDG §5 governs information that business digital services must keep available; it does not itself authorize a pitch. State an embargo explicitly, use the requested channel, and propose at most one follow-up that adds information.

## External action

Research and drafting proceed as normal work. If the user asks to send, publish, or enroll a recipient, show the exact recipient, outlet, channel, copy, links or attachments, and timing immediately before the action and obtain the confirmation required by the host.

## Mindestnachweis

Für jedes Ziel bleiben `Ressort`, aktueller Status, Beleg einer aktuellen Veröffentlichung mit URL und Datum, `Impressum`- oder Redaktionsweg, Herkunft des Kontakts, Region, Format, Frist und Zeitzone sowie redaktioneller oder bezahlter Status erhalten. Unklare Datenverarbeitung oder §7-UWG-Ausnahme führt zu `NEEDS_REVIEW`; ein verfehltes Musskriterium zu `REJECT`.
