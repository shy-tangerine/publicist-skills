# Japan media ethics and disclosure

> Sources: Japan Public Relations Society, Unknown; Japanese Psychological Association, Unknown; Kyodo News PR Wire, 2023-04-01
> References: [PRSJ guideline](https://prsj.or.jp/pr-guideline/); [Psychology Society guideline](https://psych.or.jp/jpamember/press_release_guidelines/); [Kyodo acceptability standard](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/corp/acceptability_standard/)
> Updated: 2026-09-12

## Overview

Responsible Japanese PR requires truthful attribution, editorial independence, and clear separation between paid distribution and earned coverage. The research-release guidance is especially relevant to experts and startups: methods, limitations, and uncertainty should remain visible rather than being converted into a certainty claim.

## Practical disclosure

Name the client and material commercial relationship. Label sponsorship, affiliate links, and paid placement. Explain unfamiliar foreign entities, local partners, and data provenance. Do not offer gifts or payment in exchange for editorial treatment, manufacture a customer quote, or imply a wire distribution equals a newsroom endorsement.

## Claim discipline

For every number, date, causal claim, or superlative, retain a primary source and a human reviewer. If translation changes scope or certainty, preserve the original and flag the Japanese wording for review. Unsupported traction, regulatory, or cultural claims are rejected or returned as `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
