## Overview

German outreach requires a factspecific assessment under both data protection and competition law. Public availability of an address is not equivalent to permission to advertise.

## Two-law gate

§7 UWG treats unreasonable harassment as impermissible and generally requires prior express consent for advertising by electronic mail; its existing-customer exception is narrow and cumulative. GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and Recital 47 allow a legitimate-interest analysis for direct marketing in suitable circumstances, but DSK expressly separates that processing analysis from additional UWG requirements. The skill therefore cannot infer authorization from a legitimate-interest label.

## Objections and records

Under Article 21(2)-(3), a person may object at any time to processing for direct marketing, including connected profiling, and marketing processing must stop after objection. The first communication must prominently explain the right. Record provenance, purpose, date, legal review, objections and retention; minimize data and avoid enriching profiles with inferred personal details.
