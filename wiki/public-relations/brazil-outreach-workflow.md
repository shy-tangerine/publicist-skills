## Overview

The Brazil workflow moves from a verified fact pack to a small, regionally appropriate media set, then to natural Brazilian Portuguese story angles and copy. The agent can complete research and drafting from the installed Brazil skill; this article supplies deeper rationale and examples.

## Sequence

1. Record public facts, evidence links, spokesperson limits, affected states and cities, availability, exclusions, and prior contact.
2. Classify the assignment as a media list, source request, proactive pitch, regional launch, expert placement, or newsjacking.
3. Search official newsroom and author pages, recent bylines, Knewin/Comunique-se when available, and appropriate source-bank directories.
4. Verify outlet, journalist, editoria, recent coverage, geography, professional route, deadline, and timezone. Reject failed hard requirements and mark material unknowns `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
5. Produce one to three distinct angles with public-interest value, a Brazil-specific consequence, evidence, limitation, counterpoint, and appropriate spokesperson.
6. Draft concise pt-BR copy and map every material claim to a source or supplied fact.
7. Return the ranked list, fit evidence, contact provenance, angles, draft, missing facts, and next step.
8. If the user asks to send, show the exact recipient, channel, copy, and timing immediately before the external action and obtain the confirmation required by the host.

## Timing

Preserve the deadline and timezone as published. Brazil has several timezones; use `America/Sao_Paulo` only when the source actually means Brasília time, then provide an unambiguous ISO timestamp when useful.

## Registro mínimo

Para cada alvo, preserve veículo, editoria, vínculo verificado, matéria recente com URL e data, estado/cidade, rota e proveniência, formato, prazo e fuso, recorte local, status editorial ou pago e fontes das alegações. Se a LGPD, a origem do contato ou a permissão para WhatsApp permanecer incerta, pare em `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
