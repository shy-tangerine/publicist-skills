## Overview

Media-contact infrastructure is local. A US sales-data product is not a substitute for a German media database, a Chinese request line, a Japanese relationship system, or a Brazilian press mailing. The useful distinction is what each system can discover, what contact evidence it exposes, and what outreach functions the user's licensed account actually provides.

## Germany: news aktuell zimpel

news aktuell describes zimpel as a directory of journalists, editorial desks, blogs, podcasts, and influencers maintained by an in-house research team. Its product page and FAQ describe topic, region, and media-type search, list creation, personalized HTML sending, delivery reporting, and shared editorial mailboxes.

Use zimpel after defining the story and target `Ressort`. Record the query date, filters, record type, and returned route, then verify the current role and recent work on an editorial source. The signed license controls access and permitted functions.

## Mainland China: PR Newswire Asia/Cision and ProfNet

The Chinese media-database page combines Cision media-contact filtering with the ProfNet mainland journalist-request line. Those are different workflows: database research begins with a topic and target profile, while ProfNet begins with an active information request. Preserve that distinction in provenance.

Record provider, query date, geography, media type, topic filters, source class, and reply route. Overseas handling of mainland personal information requires the China data-transfer analysis. Provider distribution is not evidence of independent editorial coverage.

## Mainland China: Niumedia as a discovery source

Niumedia's public pages identify Way&Key Info in Shenzhen as the operator and describe journalist, media, and influence research across media websites, WeChat official accounts, Toutiao, Weibo, Tencent's Penguin Media Platform, and other domestic channels.

The reviewed public pages do not adequately document contact export, field-level provenance, or current commercial/privacy terms. Use a Niumedia result as a lead, then confirm identity, work, and contact route on official outlet surfaces. Do not represent it as a verified contact-data supplier without current account evidence.

## Japan: PRONE / PRM Media List

PRONE's product page distinguishes customer-owned information from searchable public journalist information. It describes name, outlet, category, career, articles, social profiles, notes, prior interactions, segmented email, and engagement tracking. A 2024 product announcement reported more than 300 public profiles at that time; that is historical product evidence, not a current inventory guarantee.

Use PRONE for journalist discovery and relationship-aware work when the account exposes the needed function. Verify current role and recent coverage on the outlet's own site. Prefer an inquiry form, editorial desk, press-club route, PR wire, or existing introduction when that is the normal Japanese route.

## Brazil: Knewin/Comunique-se

Knewin's Mailing product describes a Brazilian media list segmented by editorial category, outlet, and region, with release composition, scheduled delivery, and campaign-level open, click, and reply tracking. It therefore combines media research and a sending channel; keep those actions separate.

Comunique-se publishes a registration route for journalists who want to enter its mailing. This is useful evidence about one collection method, not proof that every record came directly from its subject. Record provider, lookup date, filters, and returned route, then confirm current employment and relevant work on newsroom pages and apply the Brazil data-handling workflow.

## Where Hunter, Clay, and Apollo fit

These products are secondary professional-contact enrichment, not localized media databases. Use them only for a known, already-qualified person when a published editorial route is unavailable and the user's account and policy permit the lookup. Preserve the successful provider and whether the result was published, provider-supplied, or inferred. Technical email verification does not establish editorial fit or permission.

## Operational rule

The installed country skill contains enough information to choose and use its primary local infrastructure. This article is optional comparative background. In every market, verify current role and story fit independently, preserve provenance, minimize retained fields, and treat sending or sequence enrollment as a separate external action.
