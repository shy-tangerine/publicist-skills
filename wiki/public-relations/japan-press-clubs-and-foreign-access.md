# Japan press clubs and foreign access

> Sources: Nihon Shinbun Kyokai, 2002-03-09; Foreign Press Center Japan, Unknown
> References: [Kisha Club Guidelines](https://pressnet.or.jp/english/about/guideline/); [Foreign Press Registration Card](https://fpcj.jp/en/assistance/fpregcard/)
> Updated: 2026-09-12

## Overview

Japan’s kisha clubs are voluntary journalist news-gathering institutions associated with public institutions. The NSK guideline describes their purpose, operation, and relationship with news organizations; FPCJ describes a Foreign Press Registration Card that facilitates access to official press occasions. Neither source makes club membership or admission automatic for every outsider.

## Operational implication

Treat club access as a venue-specific credential and policy question. A foreign startup should use an eligible correspondent, public press-office route, partner spokesperson, or open briefing where available. It must not claim membership, borrow credentials, or pressure a source to bypass a club’s rules. Record the exact event, organizer, eligibility, and confirmation before promising access.
