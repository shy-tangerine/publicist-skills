# Japan personal data and email law

> Sources: Personal Information Protection Commission, Unknown; Japanese Law Translation, Unknown
> References: [PPC legal framework](https://www.ppc.go.jp/en/legal/); [APPI Act translation](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4241/en); [Specified Electronic Mail Act](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/3767/en)
> Updated: 2026-09-12

## Overview

The APPI framework and translated APPI text establish Japan’s personal-information rules, including controls relevant to third-party provision. The Specified Electronic Mail Act governs specified electronic mail and should be considered before unsolicited commercial outreach. A public business address is not blanket permission to enrich, export, or bulk-use a journalist record.

## Safe operating posture

Collect the minimum business data needed for an approved opportunity; record source, purpose, and retention; restrict access; and honor corrections or opt-outs where applicable. Determine whether an overseas CRM or vendor creates a cross-border transfer or processor issue. Do not guess whether a media pitch is exempt from email requirements: classify the message and obtain legal review when uncertain.

## Escalation

Consent, lawful basis, sensitive information, cross-border processing, or ambiguous sender/opt-out duties are `NEEDS_REVIEW`. Counsel should assess the facts and current guidance before sending.
