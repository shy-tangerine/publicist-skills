# Brazil data handling and LGPD

> Sources: Presidência da República, 2018-08-14; ANPD, 2024-02-02; ANPD enforcement news, 2026-08
> References: [LGPD](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm); [ANPD legitimate-interest guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia_legitimo_interesse.pdf/@@display-file/file); [ANPD enforcement news](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-conclui-monitoramento-avaliar-sancao-empresas-orgaos-publicos)

## Overview

Media-relations records can contain personal data: a journalist's name, individual address, phone, role, byline history, response, opt-out, and inferred interests. Public availability does not remove the need to define a legitimate purpose, use only necessary fields, provide appropriate transparency, secure the data, and support data-subject rights.

## Purpose and minimization

The LGPD's principles include purpose, adequacy, necessity, transparency, and security. A media record should therefore identify why it exists, where it came from, which fields are needed, who can access it, how long it remains useful, and how correction, objection, suppression, and deletion are handled.

Do not collect sensitive data, private identifiers, hidden contact details, or unrelated profile attributes for outreach. Do not retain a contact simply because a database can export it.

## Legitimate-interest review

The ANPD guide divides its recommended balancing model into purpose, necessity, and balancing and safeguards. It also states that a controller should not rely on legitimate interest when the balancing test finds that the data subject's fundamental rights, freedoms, and legitimate expectations prevail.

For a proposed media database, record:

- the concrete legitimate purpose and expected benefit;
- why the processing is necessary and whether a less intrusive route exists;
- the source, context, age, and original collection purpose;
- the journalist's reasonable expectations and likely effect;
- transparency, access controls, retention, opt-out, suppression, and audit safeguards;
- any vendors or international transfer.

This record supports human review; it does not make an automatic legal determination. Uncertainty is `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

## Operational stop conditions

Stop on an objection or deletion request, an unexplained enrichment source, a provider whose terms do not cover the purpose, a proposed bulk WhatsApp flow, sensitive data, an unreviewed international transfer, or a security incident. Preserve only what the response process requires and escalate to the responsible human.

Enforcement is active: ANPD has moved from guidance into sanction proceedings, including a 2026 process assessing penalties against 21 companies and public bodies for data-officer compliance failures. Treat LGPD duties in outreach workflows as enforced obligations, not aspirational guidance.
