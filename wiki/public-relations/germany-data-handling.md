## Overview

German media-relations operations should keep a small, provenance-rich record and treat providers, exports and cross-border handling as explicit risk decisions.

## Data lifecycle

Collect only what the approved task needs: role, public professional contact route, source URL, date, topic fit, deadline and objection state. Define retention and access, honor objections, and avoid private enrichment. DDG identity details support transparent sender identification; they do not create permission to use a journalist’s data for advertising.

## Provider controls

Before a platform acquisition or upload, check current terms, API limits, anti-spam rules, export restrictions, controller/processor roles, purpose, retention and transfer safeguards. `robots.txt` is not authorization. If a provider’s terms or third-country transfer posture is unclear, pause with `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
