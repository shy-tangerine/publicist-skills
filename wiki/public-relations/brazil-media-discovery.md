## Overview

Brazilian media discovery is a relevance and provenance problem, not a contact-harvesting problem. A useful map separates national, regional, local, trade, broadcast, podcast, newsletter, independent, scientific, and sponsored channels, then verifies each outlet's current editorial route.

## Discovery hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. an outlet's official newsroom, staff, author, or submissions page;
2. a live request or community in which the operator is authorized to participate;
3. a contracted media database whose terms cover the intended use;
4. an expert directory or source bank used for its stated journalistic purpose;
5. an aggregator or search result only as a lead that must be verified elsewhere.

A source bank is designed to connect reporting with relevant expertise. Ajor describes these bases as collections of researchers, specialists, civil-society leaders, and organizations, and highlights geographic and demographic diversity. That does not turn every record into a general-purpose promotion list.

## Qualification

For each candidate, verify the outlet, desk, current role, recent original bylines, geographic scope, format, canonical contact route, deadline, and commercial status. Keep `employment_verified` separate from `beat_inferred`. If a field affects eligibility and remains unknown, the result is `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

Regional fit is material. A local consequence belongs with a local or regional newsroom; a technical claim may fit a trade publication; a national desk needs national consequence. Reach alone is not editorial fit.
