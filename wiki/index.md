# Publicist wiki

A persistent knowledge base for earned media operations across five markets, with direct citations to original public sources.

## How it fits together

- **Skills.** `skills/` contains five country skills that carry the workflow, 36 platform companions that add per-channel depth, and five cross-market marketing skills for general growth workflows.
- **This wiki.** `wiki/public-relations/` contains articles that link to laws, regulators, press councils, and newsroom guidance.
- **Providers.** `wiki/public-relations/providers/` contains one guide for each supported contact-research provider.

The wiki is optional companion depth. Installed skills never require repository access for their normal workflow.
