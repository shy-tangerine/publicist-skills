# Sponsors

Sponsor Publicist Skills to support source checks and maintenance of the United States, Germany, mainland China, Japan, and Brazil skills.

The main sponsor benefit is visibility near the top of this repository.

## What you receive

For an agreed sponsor placement:

| Placement | What appears |
|---|---|
| Main README, directly below the hero and badges | Your logo or name, linked to one website of your choice |
| This page | Your name, link, and a short description you approve |

The placement stays visible for the agreed sponsorship period. It appears in the main English README; translated READMEs link here. We do not promise impressions, clicks, leads, or placement on skills registries.

There are no active sponsor placements yet.

## Arrange a placement

To discuss a placement, email [shy-tangerine@mailbox.org](mailto:shy-tangerine@mailbox.org). Do not pay expecting a placement until the amount, start date, and duration have been agreed.

1. Agree on the placement with the maintainer by email.
2. Send your display name, an HTTPS destination, a logo you have permission to use, and a one-sentence description. SVG or transparent PNG works best.
3. We confirm the asset and placement with you, then add it after sponsorship is confirmed through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/shy-tangerine).

Logos share a row and are displayed at up to 160 pixels wide, with your name as alternative text. A text link is available if you prefer not to use a logo. We may resize an asset for the README but will ask before changing its design or wording.

A contribution without an agreed placement supports maintenance and does not automatically reserve a logo slot. You can also support the project without public recognition.

## Duration and changes

We remove the placement when the agreed period ends unless renewed. Contact the maintainer to update your link or asset, or request earlier removal. README changes happen through repository commits; cached pages and older commits may retain previous versions.

We may decline assets or links that are misleading, unsafe, or unrelated to the sponsor's identity. If a placement needs to be withdrawn, we will discuss it with the sponsor.

## What sponsorship funds

Source monitoring, provider-guide updates, country-specific language review, and compatibility testing. New market research depends on available funding and maintainer capacity.

## Editorial independence

Sponsorship does not buy provider recommendations, favorable evaluations, access to users or contact lists, guaranteed features, or priority support. The skills' evidence standards, legal checks, and approval requirements apply equally to sponsors.
