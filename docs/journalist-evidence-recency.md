# Journalist evidence recency

Current employment and current beat are separate claims. Verify both with dated evidence.

- **Role evidence.** Prefer a current first-party staff/author page, masthead, newsroom directory, or recent first-party article carrying the person's current affiliation. Record the URL and observation date.
- **Beat evidence.** Use recent original work, an explicit current desk description, or a live request. Record publication/request dates.
- An old byline proves historical authorship, not current employment.
- A current staff page does not by itself prove the journalist still covers the proposed beat.
- When first-party sources conflict, a role has no recent corroboration, or the available evidence is too old to support the claim confidently, return `NEEDS_REVIEW` and re-verify rather than inventing a universal expiry date.
- Provider records are discovery evidence. They do not override contradictory first-party evidence.
- Market-specific guidance may require tighter evidence for fast-changing desks, freelance status, press-club eligibility, official-account identity, or other local constraints.

The purpose of recency review is to trigger re-verification, not to turn an arbitrary age threshold into proof of employment.
