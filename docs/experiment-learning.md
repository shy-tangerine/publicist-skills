# Earned-media experiment learning

Use experiments to learn which evidence-backed story choices work, not to maximize message volume.

Change one material variable when practical and record:

- hypothesis;
- market and target class;
- angle and evidence/asset offered;
- route and timing;
- what changed from the comparison case;
- outcome evidence and observation date;
- confounders and limitations;
- next decision: `KEEP`, `CHANGE`, `STOP`, or `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

Keep outcomes distinct: delivered, replied, declined, source-request accepted, independently reported coverage, syndicated coverage, and paid/distributed placement are different events. Opens and clicks can be diagnostic when legitimately available, but they are not primary evidence of editorial value.

A useful record is local/user-owned campaign data and does not belong in this public repository. Repository examples and tests must remain fictional.

Example:

```json
{"hypothesis":"A methodology-first angle will earn more substantive replies from trade reporters than a company-launch angle.","market":"US","target_class":"manufacturing trade","change":"angle","outcome":"replied","evidence":"fictional campaign log entry","confounders":["different deadline"],"decision":"KEEP"}
```

After enough comparable observations, summarize what appears to help, what failed, and what remains uncertain. Do not infer causation from a single response, count wire syndication as independent reporting, or use the loop to justify bulk unsolicited outreach.
