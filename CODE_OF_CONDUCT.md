# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, political or personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers will investigate promptly, fairly, and confidentially and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
